# Cold Steel Mix patch: Workshop patch notes

**Every upload of the patch to the Workshop, newest first, with the notes
shown on its Change Notes tab.** The next notes cover what changed since the
newest upload here. [patch-notes.md](../patch-notes.md) says how to write
them.

- **Uploaded** is Steam's time, in the user's time zone (+0100 in October
  2026). The container runs in UTC, so the version, the build's UTC date,
  can be the day before.
- **Built from** is the commit on `main` the build came from: the last merge
  before the upload.
- The text is as pasted into Steam, plain text with no BBCode
  ([patch-notes.md](../patch-notes.md#the-steps), step 5). The notes up to
  10 October, 15:57 were rewritten in this shape on 11 October and pasted
  again with Steam's Edit.
- The history starts at the 4.5.2 update. Earlier uploads weren't copied.

## 10 October 2026, 11:07

Built from `f9100fe`.

```text
2026.10.10

Starbase Extended 3.0 works with Stellaris 4.5.2 again.

Starbase Extended 3.0
The starbase window is UI Overhaul Dynamic's 4.5 window again, with smaller slots so all 21 module and building slots fit. The Orbital Ring button back to its planet, the design name and the missing scrollbars are back. Upgrade sits away from Dismantle, as in Starbase Extended.
Destroyed starbases explode again, and starbases get back their 4.5 lights, sounds and animated water. Sounds, effects and animations that didn't exist are gone.
Every starbase model has the attach points its sections need.
Asteroid Mining, Space Foundry and Space Factory get their bonuses from Mining Experts and Chain Manufacturing.
Modules and buildings keep all their conditions, and buildings no longer break at arkships.
The orbital ring hangar bay gets 4.5's hangars, and costs food for bio-ship empires.
The orbital ring shield and armour modules have a section.
Starbase sizes count, and show on the map, as in 4.5. The Ion Cannon can't be built at a waystation or arkship. The Stronghold and HQ count as the Citadel.

Other mods
Smarter Hyper Relays: Nomadic empires can build a Hyper Relay at their own waystation before surveying the whole system, as in 4.5.2.
Planetary Diversity, Ascension Worlds and More Events Mod: Unemployment Benefits and the Shroud-Warped leader's psionic unity count once per pop again, not once per species trait, as in 4.5.2.
Planetary Diversity: the AI values the Aquatic trait for species with Wet Climate Mods, as in 4.5.2.
Planetary Diversity, Ascension Worlds and More Arcologies: broken lines in several translations are mended, so the game reads them.
More Events Mod: the Under the Blanket story gives its leader their trait, and Fallen Empires don't start it.

Required mods
This patch now also needs UI Overhaul Dynamic, Smarter Hyper Relays: Improved AI (shrimpAI) and Planetary Diversity - More Arcologies. Load this patch last, after every other mod.
```

# Update check: Cold Steel Mix, 4.5.2, 18 October 2026

**Only Stellar AI changed, from 0.12 to 0.14. The game is still 4.5.2, and
Steam posted no new patch notes. All 30 fixes still build, and the patch's
73 files match the written patch byte for byte. Nothing clashes: Stellar AI
only retuned its own AI, in files no other playset mod or the patch touches.
No fix is proposed. Cold Steel's build is older than Stellar AI's last files
and needs a rebuild.**

- **Stellar AI 0.14 swaps its calendar for the economy.** Its fleet tiers,
  colony-ship alloy savings and extra research worlds started at years 40,
  44 or 45. Now they start once the AI empire's economy is working, or when
  it has fewer than 5 colonies. A new trigger, `stellarai_expansion_phase`,
  holds the colony rule.
- **Its economy checks read income, not stockpiles.** An economic crisis now
  needs a deficit, or a loss with little left. Most stockpile floors are
  lower or gone. The idle army reserve is gone too: peacetime army spending
  and `MIN_ASSAULT_ARMIES` are the game's again.
- **Its war-goal gate is softer.** An AI empire whose fleet isn't superior
  to a target with allies, a federation or subjects used to skip that target.
  Now it weighs it at half.
- **Nothing clashes.** No other playset mod has `ai_budget`,
  `inline_scripts/ai` or `war_goals` files, nor uses any of the names Stellar
  AI added or removed. No building file changed, so fixes 37 and 41 are
  unchanged.
- **Cold Steel's build is from 17 October, 22:12.** Steam updated Stellar AI
  on 18 October at 05:56, and three of its files arrived at 08:52. Rebuild it
  in Cold Steel before playing.

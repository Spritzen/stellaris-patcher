# Update check: Cold Steel Mix, 4.5.2, 13 October 2026

**Only Stellar AI changed, from 0.11 to 0.12. The game is still 4.5.2, and
Steam posted no new notes. All 29 fixes still build, and the patch's output
doesn't change. One small clash is new: Planetary Diversity's four Gaia
Seeders replace Stellar AI's, so Stellar AI's new exotic gas guard is lost.
It only affects the AI, and only Idyllic Bloom empires. Fix 41 is proposed
to keep both. Cold Steel's build is older than Stellar AI's update and needs
a rebuild.**

- **Stellar AI 0.12 adds an AI-only guard to 37 buildings.** A new inline
  script, `stellarai/strategic_upkeep_allow`, stops the AI building or
  upgrading into the upkeep of a strategic resource it's already short of.
  Players aren't affected.
- **Planetary Diversity's Gaia Seeders win, and drop the guard on 4 of
  them.** Its `pd_buildings_overwrites.txt` sorts after Stellar AI's
  `02_government_buildings.txt`. Its copies are the game's 4.5.2 ones with
  its own gaia world classes. An AI Idyllic Bloom empire can still build or
  upgrade a Gaia Seeder while short of exotic gases. See
  [Planetary Diversity's Gaia Seeders](#planetary-diversitys-gaia-seeders).
- **The other 33 guarded buildings are Stellar AI's in the game.** No other
  playset mod, nor the patch, defines them.
- **Stellar AI's other changes clash with nothing.** Its new war goals and
  defines have names no other mod uses. It dropped its `diplomatic_actions`
  folder, so the game's own diplomatic actions are back.
- **Fix 37 is unchanged.** Stellar AI's Archaeostudies Faculty is the same
  as in 0.11, so the patch's merged copy is still current.
- **Cold Steel's build is from 11 October, 12:46**, before Stellar AI's
  update. Rebuild it in Cold Steel before playing.

## Planetary Diversity's Gaia Seeders

Planetary Diversity, updated 22 September 2026. Its
`common/buildings/pd_buildings_overwrites.txt` replaces Stellar AI's copies.

| Object | What the diff shows | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `building_gaiaseeders_2`, `_3` | Stellar AI's guard in `allow` is missing. Planetary Diversity's `destroy_trigger` uses `pd_is_planet_class_gaia`, which counts its four gaia world classes | Guard lost. Fix 41 proposed |
| `building_gaiaseeders_4` | Stellar AI's whole `allow` block, with only the guard, is missing. `destroy_trigger` as above | Guard lost. Fix 41 proposed |
| `building_gaiaseeders_pc_gaia` | Stellar AI's guard is missing. `destroy_trigger` and `potential` use `pd_is_planet_class_gaia` | Guard lost. Fix 41 proposed |

Apart from these lines, Planetary Diversity's copies match Stellar AI's.
So the only thing lost is the guard.

Each Gaia Seeder costs and upkeeps exotic gases. With the guard, an AI empire
in an exotic gas deficit doesn't start a new one or an upgrade. Without it,
the AI can, and its deficit grows. Only Idyllic Bloom empires can build them.

## How it was worked out

- `check-update --notes`, in `~/.cache/stellaris-patcher/checks/2026-10-13_1759/`.
- The four new diffs were read, and the notes searched for "gaia".
- Stellar AI's baseline was unpacked from `snapshots/workshop_3610149307/`
  and compared with the installed 0.12, file by file.
- The buildings carrying the guard were listed from Stellar AI's files, and
  each name searched for in every playset mod's `common/buildings/` and in
  the patch.
- Stellar AI's new war goals, defines keys and personalities were searched
  for in the other mods and the patch.
- Stellar AI's Archaeostudies Faculty was compared between 0.11 and 0.12.
- `cold-steel-mix` without `--write` gave the fixes' outcomes.

## Limits

- **Nothing about play.** No game was run. How often an AI Idyllic Bloom
  empire is short of exotic gases is unknown.
- **Load order is by file name.** Planetary Diversity wins because
  `pd_…` sorts after `02_…`. A rename in either mod changes that.
- **Stellar AI's balance.** Its new defines make the AI more aggressive and
  readier to join pacts. Whether that suits the playset only shows in play.

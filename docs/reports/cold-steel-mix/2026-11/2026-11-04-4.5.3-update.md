# Update check: Cold Steel Mix, 4.5.3, 4 November 2026

**Stellaris went from 4.5.2 to 4.5.3, and two mods updated: Stellar AI, to
0.14.3 for 4.5.3, and Real Space 4.0, to 4.0.12. 29 of the 30 fixes build.
Fix 17 is left out because its cause is gone: Real Space 4.0.12 removed the
`sealed_system` flag from its Surveillance Supercomputer system, the one
change fix 17 made. Dropping it is proposed. No other fix overrides anything
4.5.3 changed. Two 4.5.3 additions are lost to whole-file mod copies, with
no error in play. Planetary Diversity's cyborg traits file leaves out the
new Volcanic Climate Mods trait. Real Space's star class and special system
files leave out the new Stewart's Resolve system and its quaternary star.
A fix is proposed for the first. The second is proposed for the authors'
list. Cold Steel's build predates the game and both updates, so it needs a
rebuild before play.**

- **Fix 17 is no longer needed.** Real Space 4.0.12 changed one line: its
  `surveillance_supercomputer_system` flags lost `sealed_system`. That's
  fix 17's whole change. A fresh build differs from the written patch only
  by fix 17's file. The written file is now the same as Real Space's system,
  so it does no harm until the next `--write` drops it.
- **Volcanic Climate Mods is missing.** 4.5.3 adds a fourth cybernetic
  Climate Mods trait, `trait_cyborg_climate_adjustment_volcanic`, to the
  game's `10_species_traits_cyborg.txt`. Planetary Diversity, last updated
  22 September, ships the whole file. Its copy has neither the new trait
  nor the new opposites. The game's rules, triggers and inline scripts name
  the trait, so they may log an unknown trait, and no species can take it.
  See [Volcanic Climate Mods](#volcanic-climate-mods).
- **Stewart's Resolve is missing.** 4.5.3 adds a rare special system with a
  four-star class, `sc_quaternary_1`, in memory of Patrick Stewart. Real Space
  ships both whole files, `00_star_classes.txt` and
  `special_system_initializers.txt`, and its 4.0.12 copies have neither.
  The system never spawns. The game's `is_quaternary_star` trigger and the
  Vacuum Flower relic name the two missing star classes, so they may log
  unknown star classes. See [Stewart's Resolve](#stewarts-resolve).
- **Everything else holds.** Each of the other 4.5.3 changes a mod copies is
  either carried by the mod (Stellar AI's zones), equal in effect
  (`is_multiple_star` while no four-star system exists), or cosmetic. See
  [The mod copies](#the-mod-copies).

## Volcanic Climate Mods

The game's new trait costs 1, needs The Machine Age, and binds to
`pc_volcanic` only. It gives `@cyborg_hab_preference_volcanic` (0.5) on
volcanic worlds. Its opposites are Wet, Dry and Cold Climate Mods, and 4.5.3
adds it to each of theirs. Its value is in the game's
`08_scripted_variables_biogenesis.txt`, which no mod replaces. The game's
uncapped habitability rule gives it Adapted Habitability on `pc_volcanic`.

Planetary Diversity's copy of the file is the game's 4.5.2 one with its own
planet classes added to the other three: superhabitable, tidally locked and
cave worlds of each climate. It has two volcanic worlds of its own,
`pc_pd_ash` and `pc_pd_magma`, both of `dry` climate and colonizable. Its
Volcanic World Preference trait counts them as volcanic. So the fix has a
choice:

| Option | What a Volcanic Climate Mods species gets |
|---|---|
| The game's trait as it is | 0.5 habitability on `pc_volcanic` only, with Adapted Habitability there |
| The game's trait plus `pc_pd_ash` and `pc_pd_magma`, as Planetary Diversity does for the other three | 0.5 on all three volcanic worlds. Adapted Habitability on `pc_volcanic` only, as the game's rule names that class alone |

Copying Planetary Diversity's three traits ships some of its work. The patch
already does this for its Aquatic trait (fix 27). It needs its author's
permission before a Workshop upload
([cold-steel-mix.md](../../../patches/cold-steel-mix.md#uploading-it-to-the-workshop)).

A smaller gap stays either way. Planetary Diversity's ash and magma worlds
are `dry` and not `pc_volcanic`, so Dry Climate Mods still gives Adapted
Habitability on them. That's the bug 4.5.3 fixed, on Planetary Diversity's
own classes. It's Planetary Diversity's to fix.

## Stewart's Resolve

`stewarts_resolve_system` is a misc system with `usage_odds =
@spawn_system_extreme`, `max_instances = 1` and a galaxy-size scaled chance.
It has three F stars and a G star, each with a random energy deposit. Its
class `sc_quaternary_1` and the crisis class `sc_crisis_quaternary_1` are
new in the game's `00_star_classes.txt`, with a new map icon,
`a_quaternary_star.dds`.

Real Space ships both files whole. Its star classes are its own: new sizes,
`icon_scale`, and its own triggers (`is_binary`, `is_trinary`,
`is_paired`) in its megastructures. A copy of the system at the game's
sizes and orbits would look out of scale beside Real Space's. That's why
item 3 leaves it to Real Space.

Without the two classes, the game's `is_quaternary_star` names star classes
that don't exist. The game's megastructures now use `is_multiple_star`,
which counts four-star systems too. Real Space's copies still check binary
and trinary only. With no four-star system in the galaxy, both checks give
the same result.

## How it was worked out

- `check-update --notes`, in `~/.cache/stellaris-patcher/checks/2026-11-04_2135/`.
- The 4.5.3 release and open beta announcements in `notes/` were read in
  full and matched to the findings.
- The game's 4.5.2 baseline was unpacked from `snapshots/` and compared with
  4.5.3, file by file, for every file a new finding names.
- Real Space's 4.0.11 baseline was compared with 4.0.12. Its special system
  file changed by one line.
- The game, Planetary Diversity and Real Space were searched for each new
  name: the volcanic trait, its variable, the two star classes, the
  system, `is_quaternary_star` and `is_multiple_star`. Every playset mod
  was searched for copies of the files that use them.
- The patch was built in memory. Each of its files and objects was matched
  to the game's 4.5.2 and 4.5.3 copies: none changed in 4.5.3, apart from
  its one whole-file copy, which didn't change either. Its output was then
  compared with the written patch.
- Update times came from `appworkshop_281990.acf`, and were compared with
  Cold Steel's build.

## Limits

- **Nothing about play.** No game was run on 4.5.3. Whether the missing
  names log, and whether the old overlord text boxes show, needs a run.
- **The patch's events weren't matched to the game.** Its event copies are
  More Events Mod's own events, which the game doesn't define.
- **Interface files were read by their diffs only.** 4.5.3 also changed two
  `.gfx` files, for the species view and war goals. No playset mod copies
  either.
- **Engine changes don't show.** Summed ethics attraction, the Station
  Upkeep modifier and the habitability display fixes are in the game's code.
  Mods that tune ethics or habitability may play differently. Only a game
  shows it.

# Update check: Cold Steel Mix, 4.5.4, 14 November 2026

**Stellaris went from 4.5.3 to 4.5.4, a hotfix, and three mods updated:
Stellar AI, to 0.14.4 for 4.5.4, and the Apocryphos and Kammarheit
soundtracks, which only raised their `supported_version`. All 31 fixes
build, and a fresh build is the same as the written patch, byte for byte. No
fix is proposed, and none needs dropping or changing. 4.5.4 changed one
script file, and nothing in the playset copies or calls what it changed. Its
other fixes are in the game's code. Cold Steel's build predates the game,
the three updates and the patch's upload, so it needs a rebuild before
play.**

- **The one script change reaches the game.** 4.5.4's
  `is_expellable_nomad_fleet` now leaves out waystations, which the expel
  effects destroy in place. It goes with the note on waystation links after
  an expulsion. Only the game's two expel effects use the trigger. No
  playset mod and not the patch copies the trigger or the effects, or calls
  either.
- **Stellar AI 0.14.4 is its own AI work.** It adds an energy target to
  every economic plan, stops market sales into a nearly full trade
  stockpile, and lets an established science world keep its research claim
  after a war's strongholds. Every name it calls is defined. Fixes 37 and 41
  take its Archaeostudies Faculty and Gaia Seeders, and neither changed.
- **The soundtracks changed only their descriptors.** Both went from
  `supported_version="v4.5.2"` to `"v4.5.4"`.
- **The game version shows in two descriptions.** The patch's Workshop
  description and the collection's both say 4.5.3. The patch's is refreshed
  by the next `--write`. The collection's is written by hand.

## How it was worked out

- `check-update --notes`, in `~/.cache/stellaris-patcher/checks/2026-11-14_1931/`.
- Both announcements in `notes/` were read in full. The game's checksum in
  `launcher-settings.json` was matched to the release.
- The game's 4.5.3 baseline was unpacked from `snapshots/` and compared with
  4.5.4's one changed script file. The game, every playset mod and the
  written patch were searched for the trigger and the two effects that use
  it, and every playset mod for the storm and arrival effects in the notes.
- Stellar AI's 0.14.3 baseline was compared with 0.14.4, file by file. Each
  new name it calls was looked up in its files, the game's, and the game's
  trigger docs.
- Both soundtracks' baselines were compared with their new files.
- The patch was built in memory and compared with the written patch, file by
  file. The playset has the patch's local copy last, not the Workshop copy.
- Update times came from `appworkshop_281990.acf`, and were compared with
  Cold Steel's build.

# Two years on 4.6.0 as the United Nations of Earth: Cold Steel Mix, 24 November 2026

**A second two-year run on 4.6.0, the first with Planet View off and each
trait copy shipped twice, logged 77 problems, the same as the last run but
for one game start notice and one entry from the game's own policies.
Nothing looked wrong in play, and the planet view now comes from UI
Overhaul Dynamic. No fix is proposed.** Which copy of a trait 4.6 keeps is
still open: the log now names each trait's last two copies, as expected
with three, and still can't tell which one won. With the patch's copy
both first and last, it wins either way.

- **Planet View is out.** The build of 20:12 has 26 mods, without Planet
  View, and `interface/planet_view.gui` comes from UI Overhaul Dynamic.
  The user saw nothing out of the ordinary.
- **The patch held.** The build's 65 patch files match our folder byte for
  byte, the trait files' `zz_` copies among them.
- **32 trait notices, two for each of the patch's 16 trait copies.** One
  names the mod's file, the other the patch's `zz_` file. That's the order
  the three copies load in: `!!_`, the mod's, `zz_`.

  100 workforce.
- **The 21 overlord holding errors came back**, again all at one moment,
  against the player's capital, Earth. The game's own 4.6 file, as in the
  [last run](2026-11-24-two-years-4.6.0.md#overlord-holdings-in-play).

## How it was worked out

- As for the [last run](2026-11-24-two-years-4.6.0.md#how-it-was-worked-out):
  `error.log` read with our
  [error_log.py](../../../../src/stellaris_patcher/paradox/error_log.py),
  split by time with `game.log` and `time.log`, grouped by source and text,
  and traced through the build record of 20:12.
- The build record's mod order and the owner of `interface/planet_view.gui`
  were read, and the build's patch files compared with our folder.
- The trait notices were counted by the file they name.
- The ironman save was unpacked to a scratch folder, and its galaxy
  settings, Sol initializers and Culture Worker jobs read.

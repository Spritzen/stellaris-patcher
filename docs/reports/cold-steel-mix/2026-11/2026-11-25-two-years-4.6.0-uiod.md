# Two years with UI Overhaul Dynamic's update: Cold Steel Mix, 25 November 2026

**The first run with UI Overhaul Dynamic's update of 25 November and the
patch without its Left to the authors line logged 66 problems, 22 of them
in play, and nothing looked wrong. Every entry is one seen before. No fix
is proposed.** Against the [last run](2026-11-24-two-years-4.6.0-fix-47.md),
three entries from the game's United Nations of Earth start are gone, and
two familiar ones are back: UI Overhaul Dynamic's "Could not find files
for mod", as after each of its updates, and More Events Mod's ancient
empire culture notice.

- **UI Overhaul Dynamic's update logs nothing.** Its new bottom bar,
  Ideologies window and moved ideology elements add no entry. Whether they
  look right is only the user's word: nothing seemed out of the ordinary.
- **The patch held.** The build's 69 patch files match our folder byte for
  byte, and the patch loads last.
- **Fixes 47 and 48 hold again.** Stewart's Resolve, the ruined Mega
  Shipyard and Wenkwort spawned. No duplicate system, no shader error.
- **An Inquisitor-style country was in this galaxy.** 33 entries in the
  save use `crusader_01`. No ring world reached the third construction
  stage or was being restored, so the one Inquisitor entity fix 1 leaves
  out, `crusader_01_ringworld_phase_3_construction_entity`, wasn't needed
  ([decision 66](../../../decisions.md)).

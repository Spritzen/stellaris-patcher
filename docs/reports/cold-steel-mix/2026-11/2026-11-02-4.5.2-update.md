# Update check: Cold Steel Mix, 4.5.2, 2 November 2026

**Two mods changed: Stellar AI, from 0.14 to 0.14.1, and Diverse Rooms.
The game is still 4.5.2, and Steam posted no new notes. 29 of the 30 fixes
build. Fix 41 is left out: Stellar AI renamed its government buildings
file, so its four Gaia Seeders now win over Planetary Diversity's. The
written patch still has fix 41's file, which sorts after both, so play is
fine for now. But the next `cold-steel-mix --write` would drop it, and
Planetary Diversity's gaia worlds would lose their Gaia Seeders. A change to
fix 41 is proposed. Diverse Rooms removed the broken event the
[29 October check](../2026-10/2026-10-29-4.5.2-update.md) found. Cold
Steel's build is from today, 18:14, after both updates, so it needs no
rebuild.**

- **Stellar AI 0.14.1 overrides buildings by name, not by file.** Its
  `02_government_buildings.txt` had the game's file name, so it replaced the
  game's whole file. Now `~stellarai_government_buildings.txt` holds only the
  9 buildings it changes. Its army and deposit building files are gone, and
  it dropped 32 building copies in all.
- **The 32 dropped buildings are the game's again.** No other playset mod
  defines them. 29 of Stellar AI's copies were the game's text. Its Food
  Processing Facility and both Bio Reactors lacked the game's rule that AI
  empires which don't use food can't build them. That rule is back.
- **Stellar AI's Gaia Seeders now win, and drop Planetary Diversity's gaia
  worlds.** `~stellarai_…` sorts after Planetary Diversity's
  `pd_buildings_overwrites.txt`. This is fix 41's clash the other way round.
  Without the patch's file, a finished Gaia Seeder on one of Planetary
  Diversity's four extra gaia world types would be destroyed, and an Idyllic
  Bloom empire couldn't build one there. That affects players, not only the
  AI. See [The Gaia Seeders](#the-gaia-seeders).
- **Stellar AI's other changes clash with nothing.** It fixes a 0.14 bug
  that penalised players' planets on automatic designation. It adds an
  alloys recovery plan to every economic tier, and makes its resource
  support goals optional focuses. Servitors are less aggressive. Its war
  goals stop logging `has_claim` errors for nomad ships. No other mod has
  `ai_budget` or `economic_plans` files, or defines the personalities it
  changed.
- **Diverse Rooms removed its country type event.** Its on_action and its
  event, which used two effects the game lacks, are gone. Steam updated it
  today at 13:20.
- **Fix 37 is unchanged.** Stellar AI's research buildings file, which holds
  the Archaeostudies Faculty, hasn't changed since 13 October.

## The Gaia Seeders

Planetary Diversity, updated 22 September 2026, changes the game's four Gaia
Seeders in `pd_buildings_overwrites.txt`. Stellar AI's copies only add its
exotic gas guard to `allow`. Planetary Diversity's `pd_is_planet_class_gaia`
counts `pc_gaia` and its four extra types: cave, wet, dry and cold gaia
worlds.

| Object | Planetary Diversity's change | With Stellar AI's copy winning |
|---|---|---|
| `building_gaiaseeders_2`, `_3` | `destroy_trigger`: removed on any of its gaia worlds | Removed only on `pc_gaia` |
| `building_gaiaseeders_4` | `destroy_trigger`: kept on any of its gaia worlds | Destroyed on its four extra types |
| `building_gaiaseeders_pc_gaia` | `destroy_trigger` as `_4`. `potential`: buildable on any of its gaia worlds | Destroyed on, and not buildable on, its four extra types |

The patch's `~~_stellaris_patcher_cold_steel_mix_gaia_seeders.txt`, written
on 13 October, sorts after both mods' files, so it still wins and keeps both
changes. The two mods' copies of the four seeders are as they were then,
apart from Stellar AI's comments.

## How it was worked out

- `check-update --notes`, in `~/.cache/stellaris-patcher/checks/2026-11-02_1817/`.
- Each changed mod's baseline was unpacked from `snapshots/` and compared
  with the installed copy, file by file. Stellar AI's building files were
  also compared object by object, ignoring comments and spacing.
- Each building Stellar AI dropped was looked up in every playset layer, and
  its old copy compared with the game's.
- Planetary Diversity's and the game's Gaia Seeders were compared, and
  `pd_is_planet_class_gaia` read.
- Fix 41 was run in memory on Planetary Diversity's copies with Stellar AI
  winning, and its output compared with the written file.
- Every playset mod was searched for `economic_plans`, `ai_budget`, war goals,
  the personalities Stellar AI changed, and its new names.
- Update times came from `appworkshop_281990.acf`, and were compared with
  Cold Steel's build.

## Limits

- **Nothing about play.** No game was run with 0.14.1. How its recovery
  plans change AI defaults only shows in a game.
- **Load order is by file name.** Stellar AI wins because `~s…` sorts after
  `pd_…`. The patch wins because `~~` sorts after both. A rename in either
  mod changes that again.
- **Diverse Rooms' art.** The snapshot keeps text files only. Its room
  textures were not compared.
